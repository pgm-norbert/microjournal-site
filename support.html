Got a question, found a problem, or have an idea? Email norbert.adam85@gmail.com and I will get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

Where are my entries kept?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your entries. Use the export option in Settings first if you want to keep them.

How do I keep a copy?

Open Settings and export your journal. You choose where the file goes, for example Files or AirDrop.

The daily reminder is not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Micro Journal in the iPhone Settings app, and that a reminder time is set in the app's Settings.

Face ID lock

You can lock the app with Face ID in Settings. If Face ID is unavailable, your iPhone passcode is used instead.
