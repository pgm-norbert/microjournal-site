Privacy Policy

Micro Journal · Effective 13 November 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing. Everything you write stays on your iPhone.

What the app does on your device

Journal entries and moods. Your answers, moods and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Face ID. If you turn on the lock, the app asks iOS to confirm it is you. The app never sees or stores your face data.

Reminders. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Export. If you export your journal, a file is created on your device and you choose where to send it. It is not sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party data services. It does not send your entries or any other data to us or anyone else.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
